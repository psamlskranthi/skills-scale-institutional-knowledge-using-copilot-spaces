# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Executive Sponsor

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors provide strategic oversight, secure resources, and make high-impact decisions that affect project scope, priority, and funding. They serve as the ultimate escalation point and champion the project's alignment with organizational strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Secure funding, resources, and organizational priority
- Make high-level trade-off and go/no-go decisions
- Remove organizational blockers and escalations
- Ensure project alignment with business strategy
- Provide executive visibility and stakeholder alignment

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers strategic business value
- Minimize schedule and resource conflicts
- Maintain executive and board-level confidence

### Typical Communication
- Monthly or milestone-based status briefings
- Approval gates and decision escalations
- Strategic alignment discussions with leadership

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers on business outcomes and priority decisions
- Engages with Project Managers during escalations and gate reviews
- Supports Developers indirectly through resource and organizational decisions

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge the gap between stakeholders and delivery teams by clarifying business requirements, translating needs into actionable work, and ensuring acceptance criteria are clear and measurable.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and clarify business requirements from stakeholders
- Translate business needs into detailed acceptance criteria
- Collaborate on process flows and user workflows
- Identify and document assumptions and dependencies
- Validate that delivered solutions meet business needs

### Goals
- Ensure clear, shared understanding of requirements
- Reduce rework and misalignment
- Enable smooth handoff from requirements to development

### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops and stakeholder interviews
- Acceptance criteria documentation and refinement
- Process flow diagrams and specification documents

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Product Managers to refine the backlog and acceptance criteria
- Collaborates with Developers to ensure requirements are implementable
- Partners with QA to define testable acceptance criteria

---

## UX/UI and Service Designer

### Role Summary
UX/UI and Service Designers lead user research, design workflows and interactions, and validate usability. They ensure solutions are not only functional but also intuitive, accessible, and delightful for end users.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Design wireframes, prototypes, and interaction flows
- Ensure accessibility and usability standards are met
- Collaborate on feature design and trade-offs
- Validate solutions with users and stakeholders

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and delightful user experiences
- Reduce user friction and support burden
- Maximize user adoption and satisfaction

### Typical Communication
- Design mockups and prototypes
- User research findings and insights
- Usability testing reports and recommendations

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers on user needs and feature prioritization
- Collaborates with Developers on implementation of design and technical feasibility
- Works with QA to validate user experience against acceptance criteria

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide technical strategy, design, and governance. They identify technical risks, propose solutions, ensure scalability and maintainability, and mentor the development team.

### Responsibilities
- Guide technical design and architecture decisions
- Define non-functional requirements (performance, scalability, security)
- Identify and mitigate technical risks
- Mentor developers and conduct technical code reviews
- Ensure compliance with technical standards and best practices

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and secure solutions
- Reduce technical debt and future risks
- Enable team learning and skill growth

### Typical Communication
- Technical design documents and architecture reviews
- Technical risk assessments
- Code reviews and mentoring sessions

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partners with Project Managers on schedule and resource planning for technical work
- Collaborates with Developers on implementation strategy and technical decisions
- Engages with Security/Privacy Partner and Release/DevOps Engineer on technical requirements

---

## Release / DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
Release and DevOps Engineers own deployment pipelines, infrastructure, environments, and release readiness. They ensure smooth, reliable deployments and enable observability and rollback capabilities.

### Responsibilities
- Automate deployment pipelines and infrastructure-as-code
- Manage staging and production environments
- Ensure monitoring, logging, and alerting are in place
- Plan and execute releases
- Define and test rollback procedures

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable, and repeatable deployments
- Minimize deployment risk and downtime
- Maintain production stability and observability

### Typical Communication
- Release runbooks and deployment checklists
- Infrastructure and pipeline documentation
- Incident response and post-deployment verification

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to integrate CI/CD pipelines and testing
- Partners with Project Managers on release schedules and deployment windows
- Collaborates with Security/Privacy Partner on infrastructure security

---

## Security / Privacy Partner

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Partners ensure solutions meet security, privacy, and compliance requirements. They engage early in the design process, conduct threat assessments, and reduce security and privacy risks.

### Responsibilities
- Review solutions for security and privacy risks
- Conduct threat assessments and security design reviews
- Ensure compliance with regulatory and organizational standards
- Provide security and privacy guidance to the team
- Define security acceptance criteria and testing requirements

### Goals
- Deliver secure and privacy-compliant solutions
- Reduce security incidents and compliance violations
- Build security awareness across the team

### Typical Communication
- Security and privacy review checklists
- Threat assessment reports
- Security acceptance criteria and testing guidance

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Engages early with Product Managers and Technical Leads on security requirements
- Collaborates with Developers and QA on security testing
- Partners with Release/DevOps Engineer on infrastructure security

---

## Data / Analytics Partner

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Partners define instrumentation, measurement, and reporting for success metrics. They enable data-driven decision-making and help teams understand the impact of delivered solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics and instrumentation strategy
- Set up analytics and reporting dashboards
- Analyze data and provide insights
- Track KPIs and measure project outcomes
- Guide data-informed iteration and decisions

### Goals
- Enable data-driven decision-making
- Measure and validate project outcomes
- Identify opportunities for improvement and optimization

### Typical Communication
- Metrics and instrumentation specifications
- Analytics dashboards and reports
- Data-driven insights and recommendations

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers on success metrics and measurement strategy
- Works with Developers on instrumentation implementation
- Collaborates with Release/DevOps Engineer on logging and data collection

---

## Customer Support / Operations Representative

### Role Summary
Customer Support and Operations Representatives provide operational perspective, customer feedback, and readiness requirements. They ensure solutions are operationally sound and customer support is prepared for launch.

### Responsibilities
- Collect and communicate customer feedback
- Define operational readiness and support requirements
- Identify gaps in documentation and training
- Collaborate on release readiness and cutover planning
- Support launch and monitor customer impact

### Goals
- Ensure solutions meet operational and customer needs
- Reduce customer support burden and escalations
- Enable smooth customer adoption and satisfaction

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback and issue reports
- Operational readiness checklists
- Support documentation and training materials

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Provides feedback to Product Managers on customer needs and satisfaction
- Works with Project Managers on release readiness and go-live planning
- Collaborates with Developers and QA on edge cases and operational scenarios

---

## Change / Adoption Lead

### Role Summary
Change and Adoption Leads coordinate organizational change, training, communications, and adoption planning. They ensure stakeholders are prepared for and engaged in new solutions and drive organizational adoption.

### Responsibilities
- Develop change and adoption strategies
- Create training materials and conduct training sessions
- Lead stakeholder communications and engagement
- Identify and address adoption risks and resistance
- Track adoption metrics and adjust plans accordingly

### Goals
- Drive organizational adoption of new solutions
- Minimize disruption and change resistance
- Maximize benefits realization and ROI

### Typical Communication
- Change management plans and stakeholder communications
- Training materials and onboarding guides
- Adoption metrics and feedback reports

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partners with Project Managers on timeline and organizational coordination
- Works with Product Managers on feature positioning and value messaging
- Collaborates with Customer Support/Operations on training and readiness
- Engages with Developers on technical documentation and demos

---

## Role Interaction Matrix

| Phase | Executive Sponsor | Business Analyst | UX/UI Designer | Technical Lead | Release/DevOps | Security Partner | Data/Analytics | Support Rep | Change Lead |
|-------|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Initiation** | Approves, decides priority | Gathers requirements | Researches users | Assesses technical feasibility | Plans infrastructure needs | Reviews compliance requirements | Defines success metrics | Provides operational input | Plans adoption strategy |
| **Planning** | Removes blockers | Refines acceptance criteria | Designs workflows | Designs architecture | Plans deployment | Defines security acceptance criteria | Instruments solution | Defines support needs | Plans communications |
| **Execution** | Escalation point | Validates requirements | Reviews implementations | Guides development | Builds pipelines | Reviews security | Instruments code | Gathers feedback | Conducts training |
| **Release** | Final approval | Validates acceptance | Validates UX | Technical sign-off | Manages deployment | Security sign-off | Validates metrics | Readiness confirmation | Launch communications |
| **Retrospective** | Strategic feedback | Process improvement | User satisfaction insights | Technical lessons learned | Deployment lessons learned | Security gaps identified | Impact analysis | Support impact analysis | Adoption success metrics |

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- The role interaction matrix provides a quick reference for understanding who should be involved at each phase of the project lifecycle.
