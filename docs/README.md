# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

Welcome to the OctoAcme Project Management docs. This folder contains guides and templates for how we plan, execute, and deliver projects.

## Quick Start

OctoAcme follows a five-phase project lifecycle:
1. **Initiation** - Define the problem, stakeholders, and success criteria
2. **Planning** - Break work into shippable increments and align timelines
3. **Execution** - Build, test, review, and iterate with regular feedback
4. **Release** - Deploy to production with quality checks and rollback plans
5. **Close & Retrospective** - Capture learnings and drive continuous improvement

## Our Principles

- **Customer-first**: Prioritize customer value and usability
- **Iterative delivery**: Deliver small, testable increments
- **Clear ownership**: Each project has a named PM and Product Lead
- **Data-informed**: Measure impact and iterate based on evidence
- **Psychological safety**: Encourage feedback and learning

## OctoAcme Project Management Overview

OctoAcme applies consistent, structured approaches to all cross-functional projects that deliver product features, services, or integrations. Our methodology emphasizes:

- **Clear roles and responsibilities**: Each project has a Project Manager (PM) who coordinates delivery and a Product Manager (PdM) who defines outcomes. Developers, QA, and stakeholders collaborate within defined responsibilities to ensure alignment and accountability.
- **Structured lifecycle**: Every project follows our proven five-phase approach (Initiation → Planning → Execution → Release → Close & Retrospective) to reduce risk and ensure predictable delivery.
- **Iterative delivery**: We break work into small, shippable increments with regular feedback cycles rather than big-bang releases. This enables faster value delivery and course corrections.
- **Risk and dependency management**: We actively identify, assess, and monitor risks throughout the project lifecycle. Regular escalation paths ensure blockers are surfaced and resolved quickly.
- **Data-informed decisions**: We measure success against defined metrics, use dashboards for key signals, and adapt based on evidence.
- **Communication and transparency**: Regular standups, syncs, status updates, and retrospectives keep the team aligned and enable continuous improvement.

## Core Roles

- **Project Manager (PM)**: Coordinates delivery, schedules, risks, and communications
- **Product Manager (PdM)**: Defines outcomes, prioritizes backlog, and measures success
- **Developers**: Implement features and collaborate on design
- **QA/Testing**: Validates quality and acceptance criteria
- **Stakeholders**: Provides inputs and approvals

See [Roles and Personas](octoacme-roles-and-personas.md) for detailed responsibilities.

## Communication Cadence

- **Daily standups** (15 min): Progress, blockers, dependencies
- **Weekly PM + PdM sync**: Alignment on priorities and risks
- **Twice-weekly delivery team standups**: Or as agreed by the team
- **Monthly stakeholder updates**: High-level progress and key decisions
- **Ad-hoc escalations**: For urgent blockers and risks
